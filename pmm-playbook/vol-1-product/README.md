# Volume 1 — Product Context

This volume defines what the PMM Agent is, why it exists, who it serves, and how we know it is working. It is the "company context" volume of this playbook: everything downstream — the frameworks in Volume 2, the architecture in Volume 3, the agent organization in Volume 4 — exists to deliver what this volume promises. When a build decision feels ambiguous, come back here and ask which value prop it serves.

## How to Read This Volume

Read 01 through 03 first — vision, problem, and value props establish why anything else matters. Chapter 04 grounds the system in real roles with real deadlines. Chapters 05 and 06 make the strategy testable: 05 says why nothing on the market already does this, 06 says how we will know if we did it.
