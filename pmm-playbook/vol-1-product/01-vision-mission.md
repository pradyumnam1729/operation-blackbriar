# 01 — Vision & Mission

---

## Vision Statement

**Product marketing becomes an on-demand capability, not a queue.**

Today, product marketing is a person. Every question about positioning, every battlecard request, every "what do we say to this prospect" lands on that person's desk and waits its turn. The quality of the answer depends on who asks, when they ask, and how much time the PMM has left in the week. The knowledge exists — it is just trapped in one calendar.

The PMM Agent inverts this. The PMM's judgment is invested once, into one rigorous foundational doc per product, built to a standardized framework. From that moment, every role in the company can draw on that judgment at the moment they need it: a sales rep before a 2 p.m. call, a proposal writer against a Friday RFP deadline, a CMO preparing a board slide on a Sunday. The answer is the same answer the PMM would have given, framed for the asker's role, cited back to the approved source, delivered in minutes.

**One PMM investment, infinite reach.** That is the vision in five words. The PMM stops being the bottleneck through which every message passes and becomes the architect of a system through which every message flows.

---

## Mission Statement

**Give every product marketer a standardized framework to build one rigorous foundational doc per product, and turn that foundation into a live knowledge engine the whole go-to-market organization can query.**

This mission is specific by design. We are not building a content generator — generators without a validated foundation produce fluent nonsense at scale. We are not building a document repository — repositories hold documents; they do not answer questions. We are building the pairing: a disciplined authoring experience that forces rigor in, and a query-and-generation layer that gets role-ready value out. Neither half is the product. The pairing is the product.

---

## What "Foundational Doc as Engine" Means

Most companies have a messaging doc somewhere. It was written for a launch, presented once, and then began to rot. Its purpose was to be read. The PMM Agent's foundational doc has a different purpose: to be **executed**.

- When Sales asks "how do we beat Kahua in a state DOT deal," the answer is generated from the competitive and positioning sections — not remembered, not improvised.
- When Proposals needs a differentiation paragraph, it is assembled from approved value props in the §7.4 schema, with sources named.
- When a battlecard is generated, it carries a trace to the exact foundation sections it came from. If a section changes, every asset built on it is flagged stale.

The doc is not the deliverable. The doc is the engine block. Assets and answers are what it produces.

---

## Why Now

Three things converged by 2026:

1. **AI rewired the PMM skill stack.** The Master Instructions (§1) name the reality: AI did not take PMM jobs; it moved the leverage. First drafts and volume work are machine work. Judgment, customer conversations, and story are human work. A system that formalizes this split multiplies the human, it does not replace them.
2. **The frameworks matured.** Positioning (April Dunford), the positioning → messaging → copy chain (Aatir Abdul Rauf / Zach Messler), the 7-step narrative arc (Talya Heller G.), JTBD — these are teachable, checkable standards, not folklore. A standard that can be checked can be encoded. Volume 2 is that encoding.
3. **The PMM ratio broke.** Companies run multi-product portfolios across multiple verticals with one or two PMMs. Aurigo itself runs Masterworks, Essentials, Primus, and Lumina across government agencies and facility owners. No human keeps that many stories sharp in their head. A system can.

---

## What the World Looks Like When We Win

- No one at the company files a ticket to ask what a product does, who it is for, or why it beats an alternative. They ask the system and get a cited answer in under a minute.
- Every customer-facing asset in circulation traces to an approved foundation section. "Where did this claim come from?" always has an answer.
- Launching into a new industry or persona means the PMM extends the foundation — a day of focused work — and every downstream asset capability is immediately available for the new segment.
- The PMM's week is spent on the ~50% company-level work (Master Instructions §3.5): repositioning, market entry, pricing — not on the sand that used to fill the jar.

---

## What This Vision Is Not

- **Not autonomous marketing.** Nothing ships without PMM approval. The system proposes; the human decides (§8.4). The vision is leverage, not delegation of judgment.
- **Not a bigger content pile.** Volume without a validated foundation is the failure mode we exist to end. The system refuses to generate from a missing or stale foundation — that refusal is a feature.
- **Not generic.** An answer that could belong to any brand fails the anti-generic mandate (§8.1) and does not leave draft state. If it sounds like everyone, it sounds like no one.

---

## How the Vision Constrains Product Decisions

- If a proposed feature lets a consumer bypass the foundation (free-form generation with no source trace), it is rejected regardless of demand.
- If a proposed feature adds authoring convenience at the cost of framework rigor (skippable sections, unstructured blobs), it is rejected or redesigned.
- If a proposed feature cannot show its sources, it does not ship. Provenance is not a nice-to-have; it is the trust mechanism that makes consumers stop rebuilding their own decks.
- Every feature is measured against the four value props in [03 — Value Propositions](03-value-props.md) and the targets in [06 — Success Metrics](06-success-metrics.md).
