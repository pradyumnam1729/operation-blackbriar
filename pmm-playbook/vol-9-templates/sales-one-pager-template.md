## Opening — The Reader's World

> Guidance — the cardinal rule: "Every piece of Aurigo content must open from the reader's world — their decision point, their constraint, their risk, or their situation right now. Not from Aurigo. Not from the product." Two or three sentences naming the situation this persona is in, in language from `our-customer.md`. If the first sentence contains "Aurigo" or the product name, it fails.

[2–3 sentences. Their constraint, their risk, their decision — recognizably theirs.]

## What Becomes Possible

> Guidance — outcomes matched to this persona's goals from the foundational doc §3, drawn from approved messaging (§3.2 — never straight from positioning). Precise, not vague: "on-time delivery, audit readiness, budget adherence" — never "better outcomes, greater efficiency." Each outcome is a §7.4 value prop compressed to one line: scenario → capability → named benefit. 3 maximum.

- **[Outcome 1]:** [one sentence — the scenario, the capability that changes it, the named result]
- **[Outcome 2]:** [ ]
- **[Outcome 3]:** [ ]

## How It Works (Brief)

> Guidance — voice standards: "Lead with What and Why, not How. The How lives behind 'How it Works.'" Three lines maximum, no feature dump, product named in full. AI language: "AI-native" only, agents named specifically if mentioned.

[2–3 sentences on the approach — enough to be credible, not a demo script.]

## Proof

> Guidance — one to two proof points, reference-approved (foundational doc §8), with numbers and a named or namable customer segment. "Confident, grounded, no filler." A proof point without a number goes back to the customer-evidence agent.

> **[Metric/outcome]** — [customer or segment, context, timeframe]

## Next Step (CTA)

> Guidance — one CTA, naming the action clearly (voice standards on buttons/CTAs). Matched to where this persona is in the evaluation: a first-call reader gets a different ask than a shortlist evaluator. Never a filler closer — no "feel free to reach out."

**[The one specific action]** — [what they get when they take it, in one clause]
