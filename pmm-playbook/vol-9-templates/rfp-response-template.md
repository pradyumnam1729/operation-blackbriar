## Question [ID]: "[QUESTION TEXT, VERBATIM FROM THE RFP]"

### Compliant Answer

> Guidance — compliance first, always: evaluators score compliance before reading anything else. First sentence answers the question directly and completely ("Yes, [full product name] provides..." / "No, with the following alternative..."). Never over-claim: every capability statement traces to `product-wiki.md` per `pmm-playbook/vol-6-integrations/06-product-truth.md` — an unsupportable "yes" in a public-sector RFP is a contract risk, not a marketing choice. Mirror the RFP's own vocabulary; this is also the AEO standalone-answer discipline (`pmm-playbook/vol-7-ai-engineering/05-aeo-standard.md`) applied to a human evaluator.

**[Yes/No/Partial].** [Direct answer, 40–120 words, standalone — accurate if extracted alone. Product named in full.]

> Guidance — after compliance, the "and here is why our answer is stronger" paragraph: what about our approach the competitors answering this same question cannot say. If there is genuinely no differentiation on this question, omit the paragraph — forced differentiation on commodity questions reads as padding and spends evaluator goodwill.
