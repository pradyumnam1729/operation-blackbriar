## Headline

> Guidance — metric-first means the first line is the number and what it means, not the background. "Win rate against [competitor] fell from [X]% to [Y]% over [period]; we recommend [action]." An executive who reads only this line knows the situation and the ask.

**[The metric movement or finding, with numbers and period] — [the one-line recommendation].**
