## Backlog Prioritisation Note

RICE scoring (Reach × Impact × Confidence / Effort) described in `vol-5-operating-model/04-backlog-management.md` is the recommended framework for story prioritisation. Current backlog items in `progress.md` and `keen-chasing-whistle.md` use P0–P3 priority tiers rather than numeric RICE scores. Apply full RICE scoring when the backlog exceeds 20 active items or when sprint scope is contested between stakeholders. A RICE score template is in `vol-9-templates/user-story-template.md`.
