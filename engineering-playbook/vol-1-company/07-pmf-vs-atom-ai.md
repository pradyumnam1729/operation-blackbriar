## Slide 2 — The Problem Atom AI Cannot Solve

### What Atom AI Actually Is

Atom AI Solutions (spun out of SADA Systems in 2023, built by ex-Google engineers) is a **government CMMS and work order platform**. Its products:

- **Atom**: Asset tracking, work order scheduling, preventive maintenance, mobile inspections
- **Coordinate** (formerly dotMaps): Right-of-way and project coordination
- Customers: Hawaii DOT (maintenance management), Alaska Railroad (rolling stock inspections), Chicago, Seattle, Austin, Detroit, San Francisco, Indianapolis

Atom AI's own website describes compliance capability as *"industry-standard templated reports designed to meet compliance and reporting requirements from agencies like the FHWA."*

**That phrase — "templated reports" — is the tell.** FHWA compliance for TAMP is not a report template. It is:

1. Deterioration modeling with Weibull/mechanistic-empirical curves fit to actual inspection history
2. 10-year performance scenario analysis under three funding scenarios
3. Capital needs calculation at the asset level, aggregated to NHS network level
4. Investment strategy narrative linking scenario outcomes to funding decisions
5. Annual consistency determination comparing actual investment to committed targets
6. Risk analysis for unfunded assets over the planning horizon

Atom AI has zero capability in any of these six areas. Hawaii DOT bought Atom AI for **operations**. They still use BrM and consultants (HNTB, WSP) for their TAMP. These are different software categories with different buyers, different budgets, and different procurement cycles.

### The Gap That No Software Vendor Owns

| Layer | Software Category | Who Owns It |
|---|---|---|
| **Capital Intelligence** | TAMP, lifecycle planning, RUL, capital needs, scenario analysis | **Consulting firms** (HNTB, WSP, Stantec, AECOM) — $500K–$3M per TAMP cycle |
| Operations | Work orders, scheduling, inspections, PM | Atom AI, Cityworks, IBM Maximo, Cartegraph |
| Data | BrM (bridges), HPMS (pavement), GIS | AASHTOWare, Esri |

The Capital Intelligence layer is currently owned almost entirely by consulting firms. No software vendor has displaced them. **This is Aurigo's market.** Atom AI is not in this fight.

- Atom AI's position: **zero private sector play**
- Aurigo's position: purpose-built Primus vertical configs with regulatory cycle management

## Slide 4 — Win Strategy: How to Beat Atom AI in Every Deal

### The Positioning Principle

**Never fight Atom AI on their ground.**  
Atom AI wins on: ease of use, modern UX, work order scheduling, Google Cloud credibility, city-level deployments.  
Aurigo wins on: TAMP compliance, FHWA credential, lifecycle intelligence, capital needs defensibility, state DOT scale.

Enter every deal at the **capital program level**, not the operations level. The person who bought Atom AI is not your buyer.

---

### Play 1 — The FHWA Door Opener (State DOTs)

**Trigger:** Any state DOT with a TAMP renewal in the next 18 months (check FHWA TAMP portal — certification dates are public)  
**Opening:** *"FHWA has used Aurigo since 2021 and expanded to the Aurigo Plan module in October 2025. Your TAMP is due [date]. Here is what your current tools cannot produce and what we can."*  
**Proof:** FHWA GlobeNewswire press release. TAMP certification letter from VDOT or another reference state.  
**Atom AI angle:** Atom AI is not in this conversation. If the state uses Atom AI for operations, offer the integration (Atom sends work order cost signals to Aurigo; Aurigo sends capital needs back). Position Atom AI as a feeder system, not a competitor.

---

### Play 2 — The Consulting Displacement Play

**Trigger:** State DOT that spent $1M+ on a consulting firm for their last TAMP  
**Opening:** *"Your last TAMP cost $X and took 14 months to produce. Next cycle, Aurigo produces it in 6 weeks for $Y/year — and the model stays live between cycles so your annual consistency determination is automated."*  
**Proof:** Cycle Performance Report demo (FHWA consistency determination package auto-generated from live data)  
**Financial frame:** One Aurigo contract replaces 2–4 consulting engagements over a 4-year cycle. The customer retains the model; the consultant walks away with the files.

---

### Play 3 — The Atom AI Upsell (Cities Already on Atom)

**Trigger:** City that uses Atom AI but faces capital budget requests that they cannot defend analytically  
**Opening:** *"Atom AI tells your council what you spent. Aurigo tells your council what you need to spend and what happens if you don't. These are different questions."*  
**Integration proof:** Atom AI work order data → Aurigo condition signal → Aurigo capital plan → council presentation  
**Key message:** We do not replace Atom AI. We make it more valuable by completing the picture.

---

### Play 4 — The Climate/BIL Urgency Play

**Trigger:** Any state DOT whose prior TAMP was accepted before 2024 (before BIL climate chapter requirement)  
**Opening:** *"FHWA now requires your TAMP to include climate vulnerability and extreme weather in both the lifecycle plan and risk register. Your 2022 TAMP does not have this. Your 2026 cycle must."*  
**Proof:** VDOT 2022 TAMP returned by FHWA for insufficient extreme weather integration  
**Urgency:** The July 31 annual consistency determination is the first checkpoint where FHWA flags deficiencies

### The 10-Year Strategic Outcome

If Aurigo owns the TAMP and capital planning layer:

```
TAMP produced in Aurigo
  → Annual consistency determination automated (July 31 deadline)
  → Capital needs identified and prioritized
  → Capital needs pushed to Aurigo Plan (funding & programming)
  → Projects awarded via Aurigo Build (procurement & construction)
  → Asset closeout data returned to Aurigo Maintain (Build → Maintain handoff)
  → Condition recorded, deterioration model updated, new capital need generated
  → Next TAMP cycle begins with calibrated models and full history
```

This is a closed-loop infrastructure lifecycle platform that **no competitor — including Atom AI, Trimble Unity Maintain, or IBM Maximo — can replicate**. Atom AI ends at the work order. Aurigo's loop goes from capital commitment to asset delivery to next capital need.
