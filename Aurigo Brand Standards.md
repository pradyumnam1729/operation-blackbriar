2. Logo
Available Versions
Version	When to use
Full color (default)	White or light backgrounds
Reversed — white	Dark teal or any dark background
Black	Single-color use cases only
Red	White or light backgrounds only
Clear Space
Minimum clear space on all four sides = width of the "o" in "aurigo" (not counting the dot in "i" or descender in "g")
No element may infringe on this clear space
Minimum Size
Print: 0.75" width minimum
Digital: 100px width minimum
Logo DOs
Use approved artwork and approved color combinations only
Screen / web / PowerPoint: RGB files (.jpg or .png)
4-color process or digital printing: CMYK vector files (.eps)
White (reversed) logo on dark images and backgrounds
Logo DON'Ts
Do not recreate the logo
Do not alter proportions or shape
Do not change color specifications
Do not print from .JPG or .PNG — use vector (.eps) for print
Do not use the logo smaller than minimum sizes
Do not use the logo in text to replace the word "Aurigo"
Product Logos
Masterworks / Aurigo Enterprise: 5-color logo; white version on dark backgrounds
Aurigo Essentials: logo AND isotype (logomark without wordmark); white versions on dark
Aurigo Lumina: three variants — Analytics, GPT, Risk Manager; white version on dark
Co-branding
Use default logo on white background; logos in horizontal position
Resize both logos to the same visual size
Separate with two Aurigo "O" widths plus a plus (+) sign between
Vertically middle-align logos
Aurigo logo first unless partner requests otherwise
Do not combine full-color and black-and-white versions in the same lockup
No vertical/diagonal separators, ampersands, or underscores
3. Typography
Primary Typeface: Roboto
Style	Weight	Size
Heading 1	Roboto Black	32px
Heading 2	Roboto Bold	24px
Body text	Roboto Regular or Medium	16px
Secondary text	Roboto Regular	14px

DOs:

Use Medium weight where Bold is too strong
Use Italics sparingly (quotes only)
Use Thin and Black weights to add hierarchy and visual variation
For text under 8pt: use Regular, not Light

DON'Ts:

Do not use Roboto Slab
Do not full-justify body text
Substitute: Calibri

Use when Roboto is unavailable (PowerPoint, email). Weights: Light, Regular, Bold.

Alternate: Roboto Condensed

Use for infographics and numerical representations. Weights: Light, Regular, Bold.

Download Roboto: fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto

4. Icons
Style Rules
Thin-line style with consistent line weight
May be contained within a hexagon or circle
Solid-filled, flat-style icons acceptable only at very small sizes where line icons lose legibility
File format: SVG or PNG with transparency
Icon DON'Ts
Do not mix thin-line and solid-filled icons in the same context
No gradients — colors must be flat
No multiple colors within a single icon
No drop shadow effects

Approved sources: thenounproject.com, iconarchive.com, flaticon.com

5. Shapes and Layout
Shape Rules
Aurigo uses sharp edges on shapes, not rounded corners
Applies to buttons, containers, and design elements across all materials
Graphic Elements
Diagonal line art: Overlay on dark or light teal backgrounds, or as light grey/translucent teal over white/light grey
TechLines: Clean, modern, high-tech look; should bleed off the sides and optionally top/bottom; use as section dividers, background elements, or image overlays
TechLines and diagonal line art should be used together
Essentials-Specific Elements
Essentials pattern: dot-grid with markers — use over teal backgrounds or as translucent over white/light grey
Essentials illustrations: city structures in grayscale with yellow highlights on key public works (roads, train tracks, waterways, electrical lines) — yellow aligns with Essentials accent color (
#F8D146)
6. Photography
Images Without People
Subject: public infrastructure in North America — city skylines, ports, airports, power grids, roadways, metros, waterworks, bridges, flyovers, construction sites
City skylines: horizontal construction (bridges, flyovers, roadways) as focal point; buildings in background
Convey scale, depth, layers, leading lines, interesting framing
Single dramatic objects (bridges for covers/banners) must be dramatic and aesthetic
May be tinted with teal gradient (brand colors only)

Avoid: Too-busy images, only-buildings (unless Aurigo Enterprise), studio-lit, lens flares, flash-lit, over-exposed

Images With People
Use sparingly — only when content demands it
Avoid front-facing subjects; avoid faces in focus on website and brand collateral
Construction workers on site preferred
Portraits: authentic, clean, simple composition, soft background
Groups: natural, not staged
All images: diverse backgrounds; 30+ target age group; professional attire

Avoid: Tattoos, piercings, religious/cultural accessories, stylized hair, half-cut images, harsh shadows, red-eyes, flash-lit, over-exposed, studio setup

Technical Specifications
Print: CMYK, PNG or JPEG at 300 dpi
Print documents: US Letter (8.5 x 11 in), CMYK, trim and cut marks, PDF/EPS/AI
Web/digital: RGB, JPEG or PNG at 72–300 dpi
7. Video
Style Standards
Clean, minimalistic, professional
Short depth-of-field to focus on subject
Natural, directional light — no studio lighting
Well-framed interviews with space for design elements and text overlays
Technical Standards
Aspect ratio: 16:9
Format: QuickTime H.264 / MP4
Resolution: 4K (3840x2160) or HD (1920x1080)
Requirements
Every video must end with a closing slide containing the Aurigo logo or respective product logo. No exceptions.
DON'Ts
No copyrighted music, talent, or imagery without permission
No harsh shadows, extreme contrast, or stylized effects
No material that is copyright-free in corporate but not marketing contexts
