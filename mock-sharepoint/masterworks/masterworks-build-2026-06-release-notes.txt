Masterworks Build — Release Notes
Release 2026.06 — June 12, 2026
Audience: construction management teams at government agencies delivering capital programs

NEW
- Pay Estimate Line-Item Audit (Contract Administration) — Every pay estimate line item now carries a complete audit trail: who entered it, what changed, and which inspection records support it. Released 2026-06-12.
- Field Photo Markup (Field Operations) — Inspectors can mark up photos captured in the field and attach them to daily reports and RFIs, with markups preserved in the project record. Released 2026-06-12.

CHANGED
- Change Order Workflows (Construction Management) — Approval routing can now branch on contract value thresholds, so high-dollar change orders route to the agency's executive approvers automatically.
- RFI Management (Construction Management) — Response-time reporting now breaks down by responsible party, giving program managers visibility into where responses stall.

DEPRECATED
- Paper-Based Submittal Import (Migration) — Removed. Onboarding teams should capture legacy submittal logs through the standard document intake, which keeps records consistent across the asset life cycle.
