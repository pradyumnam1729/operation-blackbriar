PRD: Grant Management for Masterworks Plan
Owner: Product Management, Masterworks Plan
Status: Draft v0.3 — June 2, 2026

1. Problem
State and local government agencies fund capital programs from a mix of federal grants, state
appropriations, and local bonds. Today, grant obligations live in spreadsheets outside the
program plan. Planners miss drawdown deadlines, and finance teams reconcile fund balances by
hand at fiscal year end. Agencies tell us the risk is not losing track of money — it is losing
eligibility: a missed federal obligation date can forfeit funds for the entire program year.

2. Goals
- Bring grant milestones, obligation dates, and drawdown schedules into the program plan.
- Alert planners 90/60/30 days before an obligation or drawdown deadline.
- Reconcile fund balances against project actuals without manual export.

3. Non-goals
- Grant application authoring (stays in agency-specific systems).
- Federal reporting submission (phase 2, pending FHWA format review).

4. Requirements
R1. A grant record carries funding source, award amount, obligation date, drawdown schedule,
    and eligible project list.
R2. Grant milestones appear on the program dashboard alongside project milestones.
R3. Planners can allocate a grant across projects and see remaining balance in real time.
R4. Deadline alerts route through the agency's existing notification preferences.
R5. Every allocation change is audit-logged for the full program life cycle.

5. Success measures
- Agencies report zero missed federal obligation dates in the first funded program year.
- Fiscal year-end fund reconciliation drops from weeks of manual work to same-day review.

6. Open questions
- How do multi-agency grants (e.g., regional transit) split allocation authority?
- Does apportionment category tracking belong in R1 or in the FHWA phase-2 work?
