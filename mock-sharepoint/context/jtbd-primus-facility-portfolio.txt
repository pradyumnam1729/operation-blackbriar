JTBD: Primus — Facility Portfolio Owners
Prepared by: Product Marketing, Primus line
Date: May 20, 2026
Method: 14 interviews — VPs of Facilities, Directors of Engineering, portfolio planners at
manufacturing, life sciences, data center, and higher-ed owners.

Job 1 — Defend the capital plan
When I present next year's capital plan to the CFO, I want every requested dollar tied to a
condition score or a downtime cost, so I can defend the portfolio plan with evidence instead of
seniority.
Signals we heard: "The plants that shout loudest get the money." "I need the roof replacement
to compete fairly against the chiller replacement."

Job 2 — Keep facilities running
When an asset fails outside business hours, I want the right technician dispatched with the
right work order and parts context, so downtime stays measured in hours, not days.
Signals we heard: "Unplanned downtime is the line item nobody budgets." "Our night-shift
escalation is a phone tree."

Job 3 — See the whole portfolio
When leadership asks how the portfolio is performing, I want condition, spend, and maintenance
backlog rolled up by region and by facility, so the answer takes minutes instead of a quarter-end
scramble.
Signals we heard: "Every site has its own spreadsheet." "I trust the data less every time it is
re-keyed."

Job 4 — Extend asset life
When condition data shows early wear, I want preventive maintenance triggered before failure,
so assets reach the full life cycle we planned and capital replacement stays on our schedule,
not the asset's.

Implications for messaging
- Lead with the owner's world: downtime cost, capital defense, portfolio visibility.
- "Portfolio" is the owner's word — facility owners run portfolios, not programs.
- Evidence beats adjectives: condition scores, downtime hours, backlog trend.
