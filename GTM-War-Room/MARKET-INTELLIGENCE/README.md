# MARKET-INTELLIGENCE

Output of the intelligence agents (Group A) — the validated upstream inputs that every activation asset depends on. Four feeds: `competitive/` (weekly + event-triggered), `win-loss/` (monthly), `voice-of-market/` (weekly), `icp-personas/` (quarterly validation). All four are empty at seeding (2026-08-06) because the data sources (call transcripts, CRM, review sites) are not yet connected — that is prerequisite 2.2 of the Master Instructions, and until it is met, activation agents must treat intelligence as missing rather than guessing.

Rules: every finding terminates in the translation pattern [customer insight] → [specific action] → [named metric it moves] → [stakeholder who owns it]; recurring signals get elevated through the strategic opportunity funnel (spot patterns → frame → shape → validate & pitch) rather than staying scattered observations; every synthesis includes a roadmap-implication section.
