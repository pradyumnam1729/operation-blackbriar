# voice-of-market/

Weekly output of the Voice-of-Market agent (A1): buyer needs, objections, trends, and messaging gaps mined from call transcripts (Gong/Chorus/Fathom/Granola), customer interviews, and support tickets. This feed is what turns the composite language in `../../BRAND-DNA/our-customer.md` into validated raw customer language — every entry cites its transcript or interview source and proposes (never silently makes) updates to our-customer.md and the objection library. No transcript source is connected yet, so this folder is empty at seeding, 2026-08-06.
