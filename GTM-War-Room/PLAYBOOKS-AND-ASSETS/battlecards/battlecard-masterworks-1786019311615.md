## Snapshot

A capital program director evaluating Oracle is usually weighing an incumbent-scale ERP-adjacent stack against a program-native platform. The choice a procurement officer actually feels is not feature-by-feature — it is **which system carries the least personal risk when the legislator, the board, or the auditor asks the hard question** (`our-customer.md`).

- **Their arena:** Capital program management, Plan/Build. Oracle Primavera/Unifier is Aurigo's most direct competitor in large DOT deals (`positioning-and-icp.md`).
- **Our lens:** Plan → Build → Maintain continuity in a single data model — the structural difference no combination of point tools replicates (`positioning-and-icp.md`).
- **Maturity framing:** Most agencies sit between "systems that record work" and "systems that help teams anticipate and act." Oracle captures work well. The maturity gap is the move from a system of record to a **system of action** with AI built in (`positioning-and-icp.md`).

## Landmines to Plant

Plant the questions that expose the maturity gap — name the lens, then let them find the gap:

- **Data continuity:** "Does your Plan data, your Build data, and your Maintain data live in one model — or do they reconcile across separate tools?"
- **AI grounding:** "When the assistant answers a funding question, can it cite the source record, or is it a general model guessing on your data?" (counters the "just a chatbot" concern, `our-customer.md`)
- **Auditability:** "Can every AI-assisted decision be traced and explained for an auditor, with a human in the loop?" (`positioning-and-icp.md`)
- **Adoption risk:** "Does moving forward mean replacing the work order system your operations team depends on?" ("We can't afford to break the work order system," `our-customer.md`)

## Landmines to Avoid

- **Do not pitch pure feature parity.** Win on Plan → Build → Maintain continuity, modern UX, and the AI-native narrative — this is the standing guidance against depth-heavy incumbents (`positioning-and-icp.md`).
- **Do not attack Oracle EAM as a competitor** — Aurigo integrates with Oracle EAM. Compete on Primavera/Unifier (Plan/Build); position as the intelligence layer above EAM (`positioning-and-icp.md`).
- **Do not name external win rates or pricing.** Internal only; pricing is not locked (`gtm-rules.md`).
- **Do not use "ROI," "defensible," or "single source of truth"** in public-sector framing — use program outcomes / capital program performance, transparent and accountable program delivery, unified system (`gtm-rules.md`).
- **No trash-talk.** Frame maturity, not inferiority.

## Objection One-Liners

- **"AI just makes things up — I can't trust it for funding calls."** → Masterworks AI cites its source on every answer: no source, no answer, with a human in the loop on every agent action (`our-customer.md`, `positioning-and-icp.md`).
- **"We can't put our data into an AI model."** → Nothing leaves your tenant and nothing trains public models; the AI runs inside your system of action (`positioning-and-icp.md`, `our-customer.md`).
- **"This is just a chatbot."** → It runs inside Masterworks with live project, funding, contract, and asset context — grounded in 20+ years of capital-program data, not a general model (`positioning-and-icp.md`).
- **"A smaller vendor is a risk."** → 300+ owner organizations and $300B+ in capital programs run on Aurigo, with named peer agency outcomes (`positioning-and-icp.md`, `our-customer.md`).
- **"We can't afford to break the work order system."** → Masterworks integrates as the intelligence layer above your existing systems — no rip-and-replace (`positioning-and-icp.md`).
