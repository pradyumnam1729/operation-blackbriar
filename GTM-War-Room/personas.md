# Personas — named buyers with pains, goals, triggers, objections

Two layers: the **buying committee** (who is in the room for a Masterworks AI deal — from the approved messaging doc) and the **segment personas** (who owns the pain per product — composites from Aurigo customer discovery, n=94 interviews 2024–2026).

## Buying committee (Masterworks AI deals)

### Capital Program / PMO Director — economic buyer
- **Measured on:** delivering a growing program on schedule and within budget with a lean team.
- **Top pain:** portfolio expanding while expertise retires; no single view of risk across every project.
- **Value prop:** deliver more without adding headcount — routine work automated, risk surfaced across 100% of projects.
- **Triggers:** new bond/funding program; backlog and overruns; retirements.
- **Objections:** "I can't get budget without proven ROI." → Start with one agent on one quantified pain; Aurigo Masterworks customers like Wisconsin DOT migrated 15,000 projects. (Internally note: never lead with "ROI" wording in public-sector copy.)

### Finance & Budget Officer
- **Measured on:** funding decisions that hold up when challenged; cost control; audit-readiness.
- **Top pain:** funding decisions rest on incomplete, scattered data and are hard to defend when challenged.
- **Value prop:** cited answers and complete audit trails make every funding call evidence-based and audit-ready; Funding Agent recommends the best funding mix per project.
- **Objections:** "AI just makes things up." → Lumina answers only from your program data and cites every source — no source, no answer.

### Project / Portfolio Manager — user
- **Measured on:** on-schedule, on-budget delivery with fewer surprises.
- **Top pain:** too much routine administration; risks discovered only after budget or schedule is hit.
- **Value prop:** PMO agent automates 50%+ of project administration; Risk Prediction Agent flags cost/schedule risk 1–2 reporting cycles before projects turn red, with reasons.

### IT & Security
- **Measured on:** security, governance, compliance, controlled AI adoption.
- **Top pain:** AI tools that export data, lack traceability, or invite shadow AI.
- **Value prop:** runs inside your tenant; nothing leaves the workflow or trains public models; FedRAMP-Class C Certified platform, ISO 42001 in progress; every action human-approved and auditable.

## Shared objection library (from messaging doc D4)
"AI makes things up" → cited answers, full audit trail. "Can't put our data in AI" → nothing leaves the tenant, never trains public models, FedRAMP-Class C. "Incumbent will bolt AI on" → bolt-ons read generic data; Lumina is grounded in 20+ years of program data and acts in-workflow. "Just a chatbot" → it predicts overruns and acts; Utah DOT projects $100M in 5-year savings on the platform. "AI pilot went nowhere" → those sat beside the system of record; Lumina lives inside it. "No budget without proof" → start with one agent on one quantified pain.
