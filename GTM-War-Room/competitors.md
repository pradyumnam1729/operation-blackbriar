## The real enemy

The status quo: spreadsheets, manual review, and legacy systems of record that record work but cannot anticipate or act. Most deals are lost to inertia, not to a vendor. Buyer alternatives in order: do nothing / spreadsheets → bolt-on or generic AI → legacy system of record → DIY in-house build → a named competitor.
