# Positioning & ICP — Aurigo

## Positioning (April Dunford formula)

**Company level:** Aurigo is a **capital program and asset management platform** that helps **capital owners** — public owners (government agencies) and facility owners — **plan, build, and maintain their capital programs in one connected system**, by owning the full capital life cycle in a single data model instead of stitching together point solutions. What sets Aurigo apart is **Plan → Build → Maintain data continuity that no combination of point tools can replicate**, backed by **300+ owner organizations and $300B+ in capital programs managed on Aurigo**.

**Masterworks AI (flagship positioning, from the approved messaging doc):**
For public infrastructure and facility owners who must deliver growing capital programs with lean teams, Masterworks AI is **capital program management with AI built in** that helps teams find answers, predict risk, and act inside the workflows they already use. Unlike bolt-on AI assistants or generic models, Masterworks AI is grounded in 20+ years of capital-program data, runs inside the system of action, and is governed, explainable, and enterprise-secure.

- **Market category:** AI for capital program management (Masterworks AI). Company arena: capital program management + asset management software for capital owners.
- **Umbrella message:** "Turn capital program insights into action — faster."
- **Category story to land:** "AI inside the system of action, not a bolt-on."

## Who we serve (audience hierarchy — CEO confirmed June 2026)

**Capital owners** is the umbrella term. It splits into:

| Segment | Who | Served by |
|---------|-----|-----------|
| **Public owners** (= government agencies) | Federal agencies (USDOT, FHWA context); state & local government — DOTs, cities, counties, transit agencies, MPOs | **Masterworks**; **Essentials** for local and regional agencies |
| **Facility owners** (commercial) | Data centers, manufacturing, life sciences, energy and utilities | **Primus** |

Never use the retired term for asset-owning organizations; say capital owners, public owners, or facility owners. Use **program** in public-sector content, **portfolio** in commercial content.

## ICP — best fit

Public infrastructure and facility owners running **active, funded capital programs** who need to do more with lean teams while staying auditable and in control.

- **Target segments (2026):** federal agencies; state and large government agencies. Facility owners via Primus.
- **Who's in the room:** capital program / PMO directors, engineering & delivery leaders, finance & budget officers, IT & security teams.
- **Buying triggers:** new bond or capital funding program; audit, transparency, or compliance pressure; leadership turnover and knowledge loss; rising backlog, delays, or overruns.
- **Walk-away signals:** no active capital program or funding source; content with spreadsheets and no executive pressure; cloud or AI blocked by agency policy; no budget cycle within 12 months.

## What we replace — who is the enemy

**The primary enemy is the status quo:** spreadsheets, manual review, and legacy systems of record that capture work but cannot help teams anticipate or act. Buyers' real alternatives: bolt-on or generic AI assistants, legacy systems of record, DIY in-house builds, or doing nothing.

**Named competitors by arena** (full detail: `../competitors.md` and `engineering-playbook/vol-1-company/06-competitive-landscape.md`):

- **Capital program management (Plan/Build):** Oracle Primavera/Unifier (most direct in large DOT deals), Kahua (closest technology-comparable; its "Noa" assistant is the nearest AI rival), e-Builder (Trimble), Procore (contractor-first; owner AI arrived 2026 and is shallow), EcoInteractive (no real AI; narrow transportation-programming niche).
- **Transportation asset management:** AgileAssets (Trimble) — do not pitch feature parity on transportation depth; win on Plan → Build → Maintain continuity, modern UX, AI-native TAMP narrative.
- **EAM platforms — integrate, do not compete:** IBM Maximo, SAP PM, Oracle EAM, Cityworks, Infor. Aurigo is the intelligence layer above them.

## Why we win — three things competitors cannot copy

1. **20+ years of capital-program data.** Lumina's Foundation Data Model is grounded in thousands of real programs; it understands terminology, relationships, and decisions others can only guess at.
2. **Built into the system of action.** Not a bolt-on: Lumina runs inside Masterworks with live project, funding, contract, and asset context. No data leaves the workflow; nothing trains public models.
3. **Enterprise trust by design.** Built on a FedRAMP-Class C Certified platform, ISO 42001 certification in progress, explainable cited answers, full audit trails, human-in-the-loop governance.

Structural moat behind all three: **the only vendor with Plan → Build → Maintain in a single data model**, plus an integrate-not-replace philosophy (win rate 68% in Integrated-mode deals vs. 41% Native, trailing 12 months per internal analysis). Anti-generic check: every claim must fail the company-name-swap test (replace "Aurigo" with "Oracle" or "Kahua" — if the sentence still reads, rewrite it).
